IVContent creation

I started making food videos in April 2026. At first I just wanted to meet an established food creator who lived near me, and maybe collab with him. So I started doing my own food challenges, taking on huge portions. The first one was a 24-inch pizza.

It was really fun, and after a while I decided to keep going with it. A few videos in, I got his attention, and we ended up doing a collab. Now he's one of my mentors.

I never pictured myself filming videos. It just wasn't who I thought I was. Putting stuff out means putting yourself up for other people's opinions, and I've learned a lot of embarrassing life lessons that way. Things changed when I stopped chasing views and followers and just made what I love making. That's how I got to understand myself better, and it's where I've found real passion and purpose. Content is a huge part of who I am now.
